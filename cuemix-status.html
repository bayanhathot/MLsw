5 · Demo order

Ten minutes, one claim per requirement, every claim backed by something the grader can see. Lead with your strongest material, not with the login screen.

  1. Live crossfade, with audio. A body → bridge → continuation sequence with no gap, and the planner's reasoning shown beside it. This is the single best answer to "explain intelligent mixing" and it is fully built.
  2. Production Audius repeat. Run the identical track twice on the VM and prove the second response uses a detected ~30-second window. The local Calm Down 0–151s → 58–88s trace is supporting evidence, not production proof.
  3. Deployment. Green pipeline, deployed SHA, live HTTPS URL opened in the browser.
  4. Forum, two browsers. Anonymous post, vote, DM with a video attachment, live notification — no refresh.
  5. Playlist upload. Batch upload with priority decay, then restart the worker and show the queue recover.
  6. Hallucination guards. Show the 6/6 scorecard only after changing the unavailable-Audius case to require rejection/rescue rather than accepting a dead pass-through as success.
  7. Memory. Log out, log back in, preferences and prompt shortcuts intact.
